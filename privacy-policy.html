Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Important: This document describes current and planned practices of The Trend Spot. Please contact us if you have any questions about how your information is used.

1. Who we are

The Trend Spot creates home, kitchen, workspace and lifestyle shopping inspiration. We are developing The Trend Spot Automation, a first-party content management tool for our Pinterest Business account.

2. Information we may access

Once the Pinterest account owner authorizes the integration, the app may process account identifiers, the account's boards and Pins, publishing information and available performance data. It may also store content drafts, product details, approved affiliate links and records of content reviews. At present, the Pinterest API integration is under development.

3. Why we use this information

To organize content, select our boards, create and publish manually approved Pins when authorized, help prevent duplicates, and review performance. We do not request Pinterest account passwords.

4. Legal basis and permissions

We will access Pinterest account information only through permissions granted by an authorized account owner using Pinterest's approved authorization process. Access can be revoked using Pinterest's account settings, where available.

5. Storage and safeguards

The Trend Spot uses a private GitHub repository to manage internal content workflows. When authorized access tokens are enabled, they will be handled as confidential credentials, not placed in public website files or repositories. We use reasonable access restrictions and security practices appropriate to the application.

6. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use providers needed to operate the website and application, including GitHub for development and automation, Pinterest for account authorization and publishing, and hosting services for this website. These providers process information in accordance with their own terms.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep information only as needed to operate and maintain our content workflows and meet applicable obligations. The account owner may revoke Pinterest access; to ask about data access or deletion of information under our control, contact the email address below.

8. Affiliate links and third-party websites

The Trend Spot may include links to third-party merchants. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may use cookies or tracking technologies. Eligible promotional content may earn commissions. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

9. Children's privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the application or our data practices change. Changes will appear on this page with an updated date.
